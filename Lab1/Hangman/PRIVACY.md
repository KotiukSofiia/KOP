# Privacy Policy & Terms of Use (GDPR)

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
This educational project does not collect personal data (names, emails, passwords, or IP addresses). 
It only uses browser storage (`localStorage`) to save your consent for using cookies (key: `gdpr_analytics_enabled`).

## 2. Cookies
The project uses local storage strictly for:
* Saving the user's choice regarding the GDPR policy.
* Initializing basic analytics (only with your explicit consent).

## 3. Your Rights
According to GDPR, you have the right to:
* Withdraw your consent at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.
* Manually delete the `gdpr_analytics_enabled` key from your browser's Local Storage.
